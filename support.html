FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

❓ How is the MSTC Point value calculated into Indian Rupees (₹)?

The platform establishes a guaranteed conversion rule of 100 MSTC Points = ₹10.00 Reward Value (1 MSTC = ₹0.10). When you pay electricity bills, mobile recharges, or redeem gift cards, your accumulated reward balance offsets the total payable amount.

❓ What is the QR verification process at the collection center?

When you arrive at an authorized MSTC collection depot, you weigh your material on their calibrated digital scale. The station provides a dynamic QR code. Scanning this in your Recycle2Earn interface cryptographically binds the certified scale weight with your user ID and mints your points directly.

❓ What are the minimum and maximum redemption thresholds?

- Minimum Mobile Recharge: 200 MSTC (₹20)
- Minimum Direct Cash/UPI Redemption: 1,000 MSTC (₹100)
- Maximum Single Deposit Limit: 500 kg per transaction (to prevent commercial fraud).

❓ How does the MST Ledger prevent fraud?

Every drop-off record generates an immutable cryptographic hash containing station signature, calibrated weight, timestamp, and category factor. Duplicate scans, timestamp drift, or unauthorized scale overrides are instantly rejected by our automated consensus rules.
