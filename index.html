How it works

A transparent digital lifecycle connecting recyclers, authorized collection hubs, green walking and everyday rewards.

01

1. Collect & Sort

Gather clean recyclable materials (plastic, paper, metal, e-waste, batteries, textiles) at home or work.

02

2. Scan QR at Center

Visit any nearby MSTC collection point and scan the center's QR code to authenticate the drop-off.

03

3. Verify Weight

The collection hub operator inspects the material grade and confirms certified digital scale weight.

04

4. Earn & Redeem

MSTC Points credit automatically to your wallet. Convert 100 MSTC = ₹10 for recharges, bills & vouchers.

MST BLOCKCHAIN · AUDITABLE REWARD RAIL

A transparent cryptographic record for every deposit.

MST Blockchain records each verified recycling submission and daily step milestone with an immutable cryptographic transaction hash, timestamp, and certified weight. This prevents double-claiming, guarantees fraud prevention, and maintains open auditability.
